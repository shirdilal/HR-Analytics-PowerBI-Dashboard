# HR-Analytics-PowerBI-Dashboard
# Enterprise HR Analytics & Workforce Insights Suite

An interactive, 4-page Power BI dashboard built to analyze a workforce of 22k+ employees. This project focuses on tracking core organizational KPIs across recruitment, equity, compensation, and retention.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools
* **Data Extraction & Transformation:** SQL (MySQL)
* **Data Modeling & ETL:** Power Query & Power BI Desktop
* **Calculations:** DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)

## 📊 Dashboard Architecture & Insights

### 1. Workforce Overview
Tracks overall headcount distribution, active vs. inactive status, and geographic distribution.
* **Key Metric:** Total Workforce of 22,214 employees with a 17.69% attrition rate.
![Workforce Overview]

### 2. Diversity and Inclusion
Analyzes demographic metrics including gender makeup, race distribution, and age groups across various business units.
* **Key Metric:** Balanced gender split (50.81% Male / 46.46% Female) with an average employee age of 41.
![Diversity and Inclusion]

### 3. Compensation Analysis
Provides visibility into payroll spending, salary distributions, and department-specific pay bands.
* **Key Metric:** $1.6B total payroll with a median salary baseline of $69,897.
![Compensation Analysis]

### 4. Attrition and Retention
Identifies historical termination trends, tenure brackets, and high-risk job titles to predict future turnover.
* **Key Metric:** Average employee tenure sitting at 15.28 years.
![Attrition and Retention]
